What, precisely, am I doing here?

A long time ago I wanted to be a _Professional Mathematician_, studying and teaching mathematics, probably wearing some
kind of tweed.

I didn't get to live that life, for a variety of reasons mostly related to capitalism and bad parenting. Nevertheless, I
persisted in my love of mathematics and I always kept a little part of my brain dedicated to it. I got older, I paid my
bills, and built a little life; but I always wanted to get back to mathematics.

The problem, ultimately, was that I lacked the instituional support of the average mathematician. I was also
undereducated with respect to working mathematicians (having spent almost no time in graduate level instruction).
However, I _did_ have a major advantage.
