# LOG

This is a general log of stuff I'm doing around the MSYW project, my other projects that may relate, and general
blather. I do something similar, usually at a per project level, with `LOG.md`. I keep notes, thoughts, and general 'how
do I put my brain back in the right state to work on this project' information there. I do not edit it with any
particular diligence, and you will instead see the bevy of comma splices, sentence fragments, dangling modifiers, missing
oxford commas and other such atrocities.

I apologize for nothing.

I don't know why anyone would want to read any of this, but you can if you like. Be aware that anything in this
represents work-in-progress and may or may not be true. It will rarely be beautiful. Whether or not it is good is an
exercise left to the reader.
