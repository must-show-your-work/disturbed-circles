[Paul Erdős](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Erd%C5%91s) famously referred to, with no small frequency, an individual
he called "The Supreme Fascist" or more often just, ["the
SF"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Erd%C5%91s#Personal_life). I, too, am a weird guy who places -- perhaps undue --
importance on mathematics as a human endeavor. I, too, occasionally hate things about mathematics and feel indignant
about the rough edges that annoy me mostly due to my own incompetence.

What I'm saying here is the SF is sitting up there with the book of good theorems, but _simultaneously_ has been
sneaking in _bad_ theory the whole time. I thought,

"Someone oughta write a letter"

So here we are.

------

I would not expect these letters to be full of great arguments, but every now and again one must be allowed to get
irrational at the SF; it's only human after all.
