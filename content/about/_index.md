I'm Joe. I like Math. I leave some stuff here for people to read if they like, and I try to work with my garage door
open. I think the fresh air is healthy.

I adopt a somewhat Heisenberg-Principle inspired approach to my hobbies, which is to say, one cannot know what I am
working on currently without directly observing me in that moment. Usually it falls into one of a few categories, Music,
Math, General Dad Activities, or whatever other current interest has consumed my attention, but there really is no
telling.
